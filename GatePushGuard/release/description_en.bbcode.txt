[h2]WHAT IT DOES[/h2]

Helps prevent crowd pressure from pushing characters from outside to inside through closed player-owned gates and doors. Applies to your characters, allies and NPCs.

[list]
[*][b]Observed crossings.[/b] Tracks movement across the actual door opening while the door stays closed. A confirmed crossing returns the character to its last observed outside position.
[*][b]Existing occupants.[/b] Closing a gate starts fresh observation. Characters already inside can remain inside and leave normally.
[*][b]Normal activity.[/b] Carried, unconscious, seated, turret-using and other occupied characters are excluded. Loading and teleports clear movement history.
[/list]

[h2]CONTROLS AND SETTINGS[/h2]

Works automatically while enabled. With Mod Config Menu installed, open Options > MCM > Gate Push Guard. Enabled and Detailed log apply during play. Without MCM, use GatePushGuard.ini. Detailed logging is off by default; errors are always recorded.

[h2]COMPATIBILITY[/h2]

Use this instead of the original Gate Push Fix; enable one crowd-push protection plugin. Works with vanilla and modified interface skins. It addresses observed crossings of closed player-owned doors, rather than replacing the game's collision and pathfinding systems.
